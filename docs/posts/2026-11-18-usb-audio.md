# Loading USB audio from the N810 SD card

The Belka receiver sends I and Q on separate analog channels. Recording them on the N810 calls for a stereo USB line input. The working kernel had trimmed exports that the Linux USB audio driver needed, so copying a driver onto the card was insufficient.

We rebuilt the same kernel with USB audio enabled as a module. The kernel and device tree fit in 1,920,120 bytes. In a RAM boot on the tablet, the test loaded the driver and its dependencies from SD, and the kernel registered `snd-usb-audio`. The four additional modules occupied 232 KiB according to `/proc/modules`. They can stay on disk until needed. The tablet then booted its original kernel. Stereo capture and interface power still need a receiver and adapter test.
