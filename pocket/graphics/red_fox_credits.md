# Red fox

Pocket adapts **Fox** from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets collection. PixelMannen created the model (CC0 1.0). Tomkranis rigged and animated it (CC BY 4.0). AsoboStudio and scurest converted it to glTF (CC BY 4.0).

For Pocket, the Walk clip was sampled into 16 poses, rotated and scaled into the viewer's coordinates. The surface was subdivided and smoothed, the coat palette was adjusted, and eyes and a nose were attached to the head joint before baking. The painted texture was converted to triangle colors and the positions were quantized.

[Original model and credits](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The winter Arctic fox is a second Pocket adaptation of the same credited model and walk. Pocket modeled a separate head, jaw, cheek ruff and ears on the head joint, added fur-covered paws to the foot joints, and reshaped the winter coat and tail.
